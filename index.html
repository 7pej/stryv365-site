Our Mission

To equip youth with a resilient mindset through trauma-informed programming in education, athletics, and activities.

STRYV365 creates customized, non-clinical trauma-informed programming designed to foster positive childhood relationships, strengthen interpersonal skills, and increase the ability to be resilient during challenging times.

Trauma can be verbal, emotional, sexual, or physical. It happens at home, at school, in the neighborhood, and in any relationship. We meet kids where they already are.
